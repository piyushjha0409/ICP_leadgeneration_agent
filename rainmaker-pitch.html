The autonomous loop

Six steps, running unattended — anchored on your ICP.

Point it at your ideal-client profile once. It searches, browses, reasons, scores, and writes — then keeps going on a schedule, learning from your thumbs up/down.

Hunt

Surface matches

Signal-scan

Loop

Runs on a schedule, dedupes what it's seen, and sharpens targeting from your feedback.

Under the hood

The build sketch

  • Reasoning core — an autonomous agent loop: search → browse → reason → score → write, running unattended on a schedule.
  • Tools — web search + a headless browser to read sites, job boards and socials; contact lookup as a pluggable commodity.
  • Scale — the agent fans out over candidates in parallel; each is researched and scored independently, then deduped and ranked.
  • Memory — stores seen companies + your feedback to avoid repeats and sharpen the ICP over time.
  • Surface — one screen: paste an agency URL → watch the reasoning trace → ranked result cards with dossiers.
After the MVP

Roadmap

  • Auto-send & reply-handling — graduate from lead-gen into a full outbound SDR.
  • Win/loss learning — feed closed deals back so it auto-tunes the ICP.
  • "Competitor's clients" mode — find companies working with a rival agency.
  • Multi-channel — email + LinkedIn + warm-intro path detection.
MVP scope for the hackathon: ICP in → ranked, briefed leads + draft messages out. No auto-sending — cleaner demo, zero spam risk.
